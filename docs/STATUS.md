## Limits

Undo/redo uses full-document copies and a 100-entry limit per stack, without a byte budget. No visible scrollbar. Layout is rebuilt (and wrapped) on every viewport update and paint, and the footer readout rescans the buffer on every paint; fine at tested sizes, but a very large file will feel it. Open supports UTF-8 only; embedded NUL/binary text is rejected. Tab width is fixed at 4 space widths. Close protection covers normal window closing, not crashes or operating-system shutdown. Long lines extend outside the viewport and can be scrolled sideways; very small windows may clip a row. Caret does not blink and lacks native focus behavior. UTF-16 surrogate pairs are protected, but combining characters and full grapheme clusters are not handled as single units.
